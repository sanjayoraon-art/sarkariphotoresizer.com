Sarkari portals reject photos with caps, dark glasses, side angles, or informal selfies. Ensure a straight front facing passport photo with both ears visible.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How does 100% client-side photo resizing protect my privacy?

Unlike traditional resizer websites that upload your personal photographs and signatures to remote cloud servers, our application runs 100% inside your browser's JavaScript memory using standard HTML5 Canvas graphics API. Your image never leaves your device.

What if my photo file size is still larger than the exam limit?

Simply drag the JPEG Compression Quality slider to the left (e.g. down to 60% or 70%), or click the Auto-Fit Target KB button. The tool automatically adjusts compression until your file size falls strictly under the maximum KB limit.

Can I convert PNG signature images to JPG format?

Yes! You can upload PNG, WebP, or JPG images. The resizer automatically converts transparent or non-standard signature formats into crisp JPEG images with pure white background as required by exam portals.
